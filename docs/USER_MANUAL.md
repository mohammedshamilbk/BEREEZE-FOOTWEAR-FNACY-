# User Manual — Bereeze Footwear Fancy POS & Rental System

Welcome to **Bereeze Footwear Fancy Cloud POS**. This guide explains daily operations for counter staff and cashiers.

## 1. Logging In
1. Open browser to the store application URL.
2. Enter your assigned **Username** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In to Cloud POS**.

---

## 2. Customer Check-In & Game Sessions
1. Navigate to **Stations & Check-In** from the left navigation bar.
2. View available game stations / rental counters.
3. Click **+ Check-In Customer**, select customer name, and click **Submit**.
4. The station status changes to `OCCUPIED` and real-time live session timer starts.
5. When customer finishes, click **Check Out / End Session**. The system automatically computes exact duration and total bill amount.

---

## 3. Point of Sale (POS) Billing
1. Navigate to **POS Billing**.
2. Search products by Name, Item Code, or scan product Barcode.
3. Click **+ Add** to add items to the cart.
4. Click **Proceed to Checkout & UPI QR**.
5. Display the generated dynamic **UPI QR Code** on screen. Customer scans with PhonePe/Google Pay to pay exact amount.

---

## 4. Recording Store Expenses
1. Navigate to **Expenses**.
2. Click **+ Add Expense Outflow**.
3. Enter Category (e.g. Refreshments, Electricity, Supplies), Description, Amount, and Payment Mode.
4. Click **Submit**. The entry instantly syncs to all connected devices.
